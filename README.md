## The game

Mara Quinn, ex-street-racer turned fixer, climbs from harbor errands to taking down the Gull syndicate across **15 story missions** in **Solmar Bay**, a sun-soaked coastal city of **14 districts** (Old Harbor, Marina Bay, Casino Row, Downtown, The Strip, Sunset Beach, Airport Fields and more).

- **Open world on foot and by car** — steal and drive **12 cars**, sail **3 boats** in the harbor and marina, ride the **metro loop** (6 stations)
- **Wanted system** — 0–5 stars, foot cops, ramming cop cars, roadblocks of heat, and a **police helicopter** at 4+ stars; hide to lose the heat, or get busted
- **Combat** — **9 weapons** (fists, bat, knife, pistol, SMG, shotgun, rifle, sniper, RPG), mouse aim, enemy AI that chases and shoots
- **Activities** — the **Golden Gull Casino** (slots + Gull 21 blackjack), **4 buyable properties** that pay rent, gun shop, garage, cash/health/armor pickups
- **Living city** — traffic, 90 pedestrians who flee gunfire, day/night cycle, rain and sunset weather, 3 procedural radio stations (WebAudio synth)
- **Progression** — money, respect, 3 save slots (localStorage), stats, and a story finale at the lighthouse

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD to move/drive, mouse to aim, click to shoot, E to enter cars/boats/metro, M to start a mission, P to pause, C for radio.

Screenshots are staged with `?scene=chase`, `?scene=drive`, `?scene=mission` or `?scene=night`.
